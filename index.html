Building Seiko SKX Mod Watches

Why I Like Seiko SKX Mods

The Seiko SKX is a very popular dive watch. Modeled after the rolex submariner, it is definently a cheaper but still fashionable way to wear a dive watch!

Modding a watch allows you to create something that reflects your personal style, and you can get creative with them too!

"I myself find Seiko SKX mods a fun hobby that turns into a nice luxurious accessory to any outfit!" -Connor Jazwiecki.
